Dissertation: do AI chatbots help marginalised communities get online?

I analysed 400K+ anonymised interaction logs and 10 stakeholder interviews across Odisha and Uttar Pradesh, studying the farmer voice assistant I had helped build.

See the findings

Voice is the accessibility lever: 60%+ of interactions came through speech-to-text.

Trusted intermediaries matter: Adoption needed humans around the technology, not technology alone.

The accuracy-trust paradox: Institutional risk aversion pushes government AI toward overly cautious design, which reduces real usefulness.
